Report methodology

This directional AI Snapshot uses 25 curated prompts across five service topics and tracks Google AI Overview, ChatGPT and Perplexity against the currently configured competitor set. It also includes a limited two-page technical review. Results are intended to identify patterns and opportunities, not to serve as a complete market benchmark.

A full engagement expands and validates the prompt universe, buyer stages, markets, competitors, site templates, source/citation sets and technical evidence before recommendations are finalized. AI outputs and source selection can change over time, so ongoing measurement is part of the GEO strategy.
